Sushi-Grade: Common Questions

Is “sushi-grade” an official grade?

No. In the US it isn’t a government grade. It’s a supplier’s standard for fish meant to be eaten raw, which is why who you buy from matters.

What does #1 grade mean?

In the tuna trade, #1 is the top grade, given for the best color, fat, and texture. Our fresh Bluefin, Yellowfin, and Albacore are #1 grade.

Is everything you sell sushi-grade?

Yes. We only sell sushi-grade seafood, fresh or frozen.
