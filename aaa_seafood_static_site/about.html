About AAA International Seafood

A seafood purveyor in Vernon, California, supplying chefs, restaurants, and foodservice kitchens across Southern California since 2007.

Our Story

Since 2007, AAA International Seafood has been sourcing and distributing fresh and frozen seafood to restaurants, sushi bars, markets, and wholesale customers throughout Southern California. Operating out of Vernon, CA, we've built our business on consistency: the same sushi-grade tuna, the same cold-chain standards, and the same direct relationships with our customers, delivery after delivery.

What started as a wholesale tuna supplier has grown into a full-line seafood purveyor carrying more than 100 products, from tuna to uni.

What We Do

We supply chefs, restaurants, sushi bars, and foodservice kitchens with the highest-grade tuna and fresh and frozen fish, from bluefin to king salmon, madai, and kinmedai. We maintain direct relationships with the farms and fisheries we source from, so quality and traceability are controlled from the water to your kitchen.

Our team works directly with buyers and chefs so the right cuts and quantities arrive on schedule, with every order handled under HACCP-aligned, cold-chain protocols from the dock to your door.
