About AAA International Seafood

A seafood purveyor in Vernon, California, supplying chefs, restaurants, and foodservice kitchens across Southern California since 2007.

Our Story

Since 2007, AAA International Seafood has been sourcing and distributing fresh and frozen seafood to restaurants, sushi bars, markets, and wholesale customers throughout Southern California. Operating out of Vernon, CA, we've built our business on consistency: the same sushi-grade tuna, the same cold-chain standards, and the same direct relationships with our customers, delivery after delivery.

What started as a wholesale tuna supplier has grown into a full-line seafood purveyor carrying more than 100 products, from tuna to Uni.

What We Do

We supply chefs, restaurants, sushi bars, and foodservice kitchens with the highest-grade tuna and fresh and frozen fish, from Bluefin to King Salmon, Madai (Red Sea Bream), and Kinmedai (Golden Eye Snapper). We maintain direct relationships with the farms and fisheries we source from, so quality and traceability are controlled from the water to your kitchen.

Our team works directly with buyers and chefs so the right products and quantities arrive on schedule, with every order handled under HACCP-aligned, cold-chain protocols from the dock to your door.

What Sets Us Apart

Sushi-grade, nothing less

We only sell sushi-grade seafood. Every product, fresh or frozen, meets the standard a sushi counter expects.

Tuna is where we started

We opened in 2007 as a tuna supplier, and tuna is still our specialty: the highest-grade Bluefin, Yellowfin, and Albacore, held to one standard on every order.

A frozen label of our own

AAA Branded Frozen Tuna is our own line, from loins and saku to ground tuna, so the frozen side of your menu is as dependable as the fresh.

Sourced worldwide, delivered locally

Bluefin from Mexico and Spain and Uni from Santa Barbara and Japan, all handled out of Vernon and delivered on our routes across Southern California.
