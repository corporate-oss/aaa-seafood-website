Fresh Fish

Dock-Fresh Daily

The fresh fish we carry, with how chefs use each one.

Salmon & King Salmon

We carry both Salmon and King Salmon. King Salmon is prized for its high fat content and silky texture, a favorite for crudo as well as the grill.

  • Includes Salmon and King Salmon
  • Flavor & texture Rich and buttery; King Salmon is the richest
  • Best for Crudo, sashimi, pan-roasted, grilled

Branzino

Branzino, the European Sea Bass, has mild, sweet white flesh and thin skin that crisps well. A Mediterranean classic, often roasted or grilled whole.

  • Also known as European Sea Bass
  • Flavor & texture Mild, sweet, delicate
  • Best for Whole-roasted, grilled, pan-seared

Striped Bass

A firm, flaky white fish with a clean, mildly sweet flavor that holds up well in the pan and on the grill.

  • Flavor & texture Firm, flaky, mildly sweet
  • Best for Pan-roasted, grilled, crudo

Madai (Red Sea Bream)

Madai is one of Japan’s most celebrated fish, with delicate, sweet white flesh and striking pink-red skin.

  • Also known as Red Sea Bream
  • Flavor & texture Delicate, sweet, firm
  • Best for Crudo, sashimi, salt-baked whole

Kanpachi (Amberjack)

Kanpachi is clean and rich, with a firm bite and a buttery finish, a favorite for crudo and sashimi.

  • Also known as Amberjack
  • Flavor & texture Clean, rich, firm
  • Best for Crudo, sashimi, nigiri

Shima Aji (Striped Jack)

Shima Aji is prized at sushi counters for its firm, clean, subtly sweet flesh.

  • Also known as Striped Jack
  • Flavor & texture Firm, clean, subtly sweet
  • Best for Sashimi, nigiri, crudo

Kinmedai (Golden Eye Snapper)

Kinmedai has bright red skin over rich, tender white flesh. Chefs often sear the skin lightly before slicing.

  • Also known as Golden Eye Snapper
  • Flavor & texture Rich, tender
  • Best for Seared-skin sashimi, nigiri

Hirame (Flounder)

Hirame is a lean, delicate flatfish with a clean flavor, traditionally sliced thin for sashimi.

  • Also known as Flounder
  • Flavor & texture Lean, delicate, clean
  • Best for Thin-sliced sashimi, crudo

New Zealand Tai (Snapper)

New Zealand Tai, known there as Snapper, is a mild, sweet Sea Bream that works raw or roasted whole.

  • Also known as New Zealand Snapper
  • Flavor & texture Mild, sweet, firm
  • Best for Crudo, sashimi, whole-roasted

Kurodai (Black Sea Bream)

Kurodai has firm white flesh with a clean, mild flavor, for sashimi or the grill.

  • Also known as Black Sea Bream
  • Flavor & texture Firm, clean, mild
  • Best for Sashimi, grilled

Aji (Horse Mackerel)

Aji is small and full of flavor, a classic silver-skinned fish at the sushi bar.

  • Also known as Horse Mackerel
  • Flavor & texture Rich, full-flavored
  • Best for Sashimi, nigiri, fried
